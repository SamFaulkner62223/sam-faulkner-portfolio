# Healthcare AI Analytics — Evidence Record

## Purpose
Answer supported healthcare business questions from prepared, validated assets while refusing unsupported interpretations.

## Work documented
Profiling and cleaning feed semantic definitions, control-total checks and curated knowledge assets. Governed Q&A classifies answerability before producing charts and reviewable reports.

## Documented examples and outcomes
The saved case study describes a supported facility case-mix-index answer with benchmark context, chart controls and a supporting table. It also records an unsupported mortality question being refused because required outcome fields were absent, accompanied by a research plan rather than an invented answer.
The source reports passing project tests, offline guardrail checks, row-count reconciliation and duplicate-identifier checks. No raw execution log or fresh test run is included in this evidence package.

## What this supports
A documented readiness-before-AI approach and explicit separation of answerable questions from unsupported claims.

## Source basis
Professional Portfolio Platform, “Agentic AI Healthcare Analytics Platform” public-safe case study, dated June 29, 2026; existing healthcare architecture diagram.

## Limits
Graduate prototype, not a production clinical, diagnosis, reimbursement or financial decision system. This record does not establish clinical accuracy or savings. Raw healthcare data and private course files are not published.
