# AI Governance and Evidence - Cross-Project Record

## Purpose
Keep decisions, actions and professional claims connected to evidence through a repeatable record, review and update process. This is a capability spanning existing projects, not another application.

## MIRA: approval and continuity
The saved fixed-task pilot assessment documents explicit approval, a separate execution action and verified completion. A later saved recovery report records one automated test passing for checkpoint resume, helper/actor continuation, approval enforcement and replay without duplicate semantic rows. These are bounded local results, not proof of live switching between external LLMs.

## Healthcare: answerability boundaries
The saved case study describes answers based on validated assets and a refusal when required outcome fields were absent. The refusal supplied a research plan rather than inventing an answer. The architecture separates data preparation and validation from AI interpretation.

## PPP: evidence indexing and claim coverage
Knowledge Core documentation defines relationships among capabilities, projects, claims, artifacts and evidence. Authority, freshness, confidence, provenance and visibility rules qualify their use. Its documented coverage reports flag missing, weak, restricted or draft support. Source references help locate evidence without copying private document contents into a public page.

## What this demonstrates
This case study brings together governance practices demonstrated across MIRA, Healthcare AI Analytics, and PPP: human approval, checkpoint recovery, validated answers, and traceable links between claims and supporting evidence. Sam directs architecture, governance and review with AI-assisted implementation.

## Source basis
MIRA live pilot assessment and saved ingestion-recovery test report; Healthcare AI Platform case study; PPP Knowledge Core README, portfolio relationship model and Public-Safe Rules. The individual portfolio cards retain their project-specific evidence reports.

## Scope
Evidence comes from documented prototypes and recorded local test results. Production-wide validation remains a future step.
