# Crop Bot - Regional Yield Screening Evidence

## Contribution
Samuel Faulkner built and checked an interactive corn-hybrid screening application as a graduate hackathon project with team Crop Circuits. The work connected data reconciliation, interactive decision support and supplied-model inference replay.

## Recorded work
The application exposes regional predictions, screening thresholds, review flags and downloadable candidate rows. It uses supplied model checkpoints rather than training a new model inside the application.

## Recorded outcomes
Reconciled region labels and checked live inference against supplied predictions. Reproduction of supplied predictions is distinct from predictive accuracy against future yields.

## Evaluation discipline
The workflow makes retrospective evaluation, baseline behavior and regional limitations visible to reviewers rather than presenting a single headline accuracy claim.

## Source and scope
Prepared from the curated PPP Crop Bot Regional Yield Screening experience record. This is a graduate hackathon prototype with historical validation evidence. No model tests were rerun for this portfolio update. Protected competition data and model checkpoints remain private.
